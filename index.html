Needs a connection exactly once

The app ships without crop models, so the first launch downloads the one you pick. After that the whole farmer loop — forecast, photo, diagnosis, treatment advice — runs on the phone with the radio off.

First run

  1. Install and open the app.
  2. Choose a crop. Potato is the farmer app; the two scans are lab tools that locate plants and diagnose nothing.
  3. Wait for the download to finish, then turn the radio off and try it.

What works with no network

Weather risk forecasting, the scouting alert, photo diagnosis, treatment advice and both lab scanners. What needs a connection: downloading a new crop model, and cross-farm outbreak data from other farmers' devices.
